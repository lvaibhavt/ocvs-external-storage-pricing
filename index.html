Read before quoting these figures

  • Indicative only. Public list prices, no discounts, no commitments. Always confirm on the provider's own calculator before sharing with a customer.
  • Capacity is what you provision, not what you consume after dedupe or compression. Partner products are often quoted as effective capacity instead.
  • Billing units differ. OCI and AWS bill per decimal GB (1 TiB = 1,099.51 GB); Azure and Google bill per binary GiB (1 TiB = 1,024 GiB). The tool converts for you.
  • Storage only. Excludes VMware nodes, networking, egress, backup, support and taxes. For node pricing use the OCVS pricing comparison tool.
  • Not included in the monthly figure: FSx throughput capacity and provisioned IOPS, ANF Flexible throughput, Filestore custom performance, and OCI HPMT performance units. Each is called out in the notes.
