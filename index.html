    Read before quoting these figures

    • Indicative only. Public list prices, no discounts or commitments. Confirm on the provider's own calculator before sharing with a customer.
    • 1 TiB = 1,024 billed units, as in each provider's calculator. OCI and AWS call the unit "GB", Azure and Google "GiB". Check: 1,024 GB OCI Block Volume at Balanced = 1,024 × $0.0425 = $43.52/month.
    • Hours per month (default 730) applies to the per-hour services: Azure NetApp Files, Google Cloud NetApp Volumes and Filestore. OCI, Elastic SAN and FSx quote flat monthly rates.
    • Capacity is provisioned capacity, not capacity after dedupe or compression. Partner products are usually quoted as effective capacity instead.
    • Not included in the price: FSx throughput capacity and extra SSD IOPS, ANF Flexible throughput, Filestore custom performance, OCI HPMT performance units. Each is flagged in the notes.
    • Storage only: no VMware nodes, networking, egress, backup, support or taxes.
